GOD'S EYE // UAP

Socorro landing (Lonnie Zamora)

24 April 1964 · Socorro, New Mexico · Landing / trace case
UNRESOLVED
No accepted explanation

Chasing a speeder south of Socorro, Sgt. Lonnie Zamora heard a roar and saw flame, drove toward it and saw a whitish oval object in a gully with two small figures in white beside it. The object rose on a blue-orange flame and left. Investigators found scorched brush and four depressions.

Explanation

Project Blue Book listed the case as "unknown" — one of its best-known. Proposed explanations include a lunar-lander test and a student hoax; none is confirmed.

What was seen

Shape: Shiny egg / "O" shape on legs; red insignia
Witnesses: Police Sgt. Lonnie Zamora
Duration: A few minutes
