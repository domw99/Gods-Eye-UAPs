Cláudio — Military Police report

19 November 2008 · Cláudio, Minas Gerais · Close encounter

Over the night of November 19–20, 2008, officers of the Minas Gerais Military Police said they chased luminous humanoid figures and photographed lights in the sky around the town of Cláudio. Seven officers signed the report, which includes three photographs and a police incident record.
