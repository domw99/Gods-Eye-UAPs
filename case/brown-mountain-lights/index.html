Brown Mountain lights

13 September 1913 · Brown Mountain, Pisgah National Forest, North Carolina · Recurring lights
EXPLAINED
Mundane explanation widely accepted or officially assessed
Open on the 3D globe →

In 1913 the Charlotte Observer reported lights rising over Brown Mountain in the Blue Ridge, during the years when electric lighting and railways were reaching the area. Two U.S. Geological Survey investigations followed. Legends of lights "seen by the Cherokee" were added later. The lights are still watched from roadside overlooks.

Explanation

In 1922, U.S. Geological Survey geologist George R. Mansfield used maps and an alidade to show the lights were train headlights, car headlights and brush fires in the valleys beyond the ridge. A 1913 USGS look had already pointed to locomotive headlights.

What was seen

Shape: Lights rising above the ridge
Witnesses: Visitors to overlooks such as Wiseman's View
Duration: Recurring
