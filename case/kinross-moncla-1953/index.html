Kinross F-89 disappearance over Lake Superior

23 November 1953 · Lake Superior, off the Keweenaw Peninsula, Michigan · Military encounter
UNRESOLVED
No accepted explanation

Air defense radar picked up an unidentified target over the restricted airspace near the Soo Locks. An F-89C Scorpion was scrambled from Kinross AFB, flown by 1st Lt. Felix Moncla with radar operator 2nd Lt. Robert Wilson. Ground controllers watched the fighter close on the target over Lake Superior at about 8,000 ft, until the two returns merged into one. The F-89 never answered again, and neither the aircraft nor its crew was ever found.

Explanation

The Air Force said the target was a Royal Canadian Air Force C-47 flying off course and that the F-89 probably crashed after the pilot became disoriented (vertigo). The RCAF denied any intercept took place, and no wreckage of the F-89 has ever been found.

What was seen

Shape: Unidentified radar target
Witnesses: Ground-control radar operators
Duration: About 30 minutes
