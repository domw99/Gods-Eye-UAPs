Trindade Island photographs

16 January 1958 · Trindade Island, South Atlantic · Photo / video case
DISPUTED
Explanation proposed but contested
Open on the 3D globe →

As the Navy survey ship Almirante Saldanha prepared to leave Trindade during the International Geophysical Year, people on deck saw a ringed disc approach from the east, pass over Desejado Peak, turn sharply and leave to the north-west. Photographer Almiro Baraúna took four frames; President Kubitschek allowed their release.

Explanation

The Brazilian Navy examined the negatives and did not declare a hoax; skeptics argue photographer Almiro Baraúna faked them.

What was seen

Shape: Saturn-like disc with a ring
Witnesses: Crew and scientists aboard the Almirante Saldanha
Duration: Under a minute
