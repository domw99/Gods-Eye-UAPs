Celestial phenomenon over Nuremberg

14 April 1561 · Nuremberg, Holy Roman Empire (Germany) · Historical account
EXPLAINED
Mundane explanation widely accepted or officially assessed
Open on the 3D globe →

A broadsheet by Hans Glaser describes a dawn "battle" in the sky: globes, crosses and cylinders that moved and fought before falling to the ground with smoke. It is a staple of UFO histories and an example of how atmospheric optics were recorded before modern science.

Explanation

Usually interpreted as a spectacular sun-halo display (parhelia, arcs) described in religious terms.

What was seen

Shape: Spheres, crosses, tubes and a black spear
Witnesses: Citizens of Nuremberg
Duration: About an hour at sunrise
