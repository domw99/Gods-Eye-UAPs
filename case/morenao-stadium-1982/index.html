Morenão stadium sighting

6 March 1982 · Estádio Morenão, Campo Grande, Mato Grosso do Sul · Mass sighting
UNRESOLVED
No accepted explanation
Open on the 3D globe →

During a Brazilian championship match between Operário and Vasco da Gama, a crowd of more than 24,000 saw unidentified objects over the stadium. Players, including Cocada, and referee José de Assis Aragão described them. Among the spectators was Ademar José Gevaerd, who went on to become one of Brazil's best-known UFO researchers.

What was seen

Shape: Unidentified lights
Witnesses: More than 24,000 spectators, players and the referee
Duration: Minutes
