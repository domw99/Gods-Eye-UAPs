Two objects near Columbus, Ohio, on a military infrared sensor

1 November 2022 · The vicinity of Columbus, Ohio, as the uploader’s title gives it (exact place withheld) · Photo / video case
UNASSESSED
Released without an official determination

Among the clips AARO found on a classified network after the congressional request of March 2026 was one titled “IIR 1 655 S0053 23/Several Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon Encountered In The Vicinity of Columbus OH”, uploaded in March 2023. AARO assesses that it was filmed by an infrared sensor on a U.S. military platform in the United States in November 2022. The sensor centres a small area of contrast and cycles through zoom and contrast modes; a second object enters from the top and crosses the frame. The first seems to rotate, which AARO puts down to the sensor’s changing angle. It was released in May 2026 as DOW-UAP-PR073.

Explanation

Released in May 2026 without any finding. AARO says the clip is likely from an infrared sensor on a U.S. military platform within Northern Command in November 2022, and that the batch lacks a substantiated chain of custody; it notes that one object only appears to rotate because of the sensor’s changing orientation.

What was seen

Shape: A small area of contrast, then a second one crossing the view
Witnesses: The crew of a U.S. military platform with an infrared sensor
Duration: About 1½ minutes of footage
