Lake Cote aerial survey photograph

4 September 1971 · Laguna de Cote, Costa Rica · Photo / video case
UNRESOLVED
No accepted explanation

An Instituto Geográfico Nacional aerial-survey camera flying at about 10,000 ft photographed Lake Cote for mapping. One frame shows a disc-like object over the water that the crew never saw; the adjacent frames do not show it.

Explanation

Taken by the government’s Instituto Geográfico Nacional mapping aircraft; the object was not noticed at the time. Analyses have not produced an accepted identification.

What was seen

Shape: Metallic disc over the lake
Witnesses: None at the time (found on the negative)
Duration: Single survey frame
