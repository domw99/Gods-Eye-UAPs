Colares "chupa-chupa" & Operação Prato

15 October 1977 · Colares Island, Pará · Military encounter
DISPUTED
Explanation proposed but contested
Open on the 3D globe →

Residents of Colares reported lights that fired beams and left burn-like marks. The mayor asked for help, and the Brazilian Air Force ran Operação Prato under Capt. Uyrangê Hollanda, photographing lights over several months. Hollanda gave a dramatic interview two decades later.

Explanation

The Brazilian Air Force closed Operação Prato after about four months, reporting no unusual phenomena; its files and photos are in the Arquivo Nacional.

What was seen

Shape: Beams of light, "chupa-chupa" lights
Witnesses: Islanders; Brazilian Air Force team
Duration: Months in 1977
