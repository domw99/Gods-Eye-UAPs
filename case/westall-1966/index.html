Westall school sighting

6 April 1966 · Westall High School, Clayton South, Melbourne · Mass sighting
DISPUTED
Explanation proposed but contested
Open on the 3D globe →

Students and staff watched a silvery disc descend behind trees into a paddock known as The Grange, then rise and leave while light aircraft circled. Several students said they saw a flattened circle in the grass.

Explanation

Researchers have proposed a stray HIBAL cosmic-ray research balloon launched from Mildura, trailed by light aircraft.

What was seen

Shape: Silvery disc; descended into a paddock
Witnesses: 200+ students and teachers
Duration: ~20 minutes
