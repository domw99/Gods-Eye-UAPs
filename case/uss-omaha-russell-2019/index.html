USS Omaha sphere & USS Russell "pyramids"

15 July 2019 · Off San Diego, California · Military encounter
DISPUTED
Explanation proposed but contested
Open on the 3D globe →

During July 2019, several Navy ships off Southern California reported drone-like objects around them at night. A CIC video from USS Omaha shows a sphere flying over the ocean and apparently descending into the water; night-vision footage from USS Russell shows blinking pyramid-shaped lights. Both leaked in 2021 and were confirmed as authentic Navy footage.

Explanation

The Pentagon confirmed the leaked clips were taken by Navy personnel. Analysts attribute the "pyramids" to the triangular aperture of a night-vision lens (bokeh) on aircraft/stars, and the Omaha sphere to a distant aircraft or balloon.

What was seen

Shape: Spherical object descending into the sea; flashing pyramid shapes
Witnesses: Crew of USS Omaha, USS Russell and other ships
Duration: Several nights in July 2019
