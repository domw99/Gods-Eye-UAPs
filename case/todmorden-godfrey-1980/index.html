GOD'S EYE // UAP

Todmorden — PC Alan Godfrey

29 November 1980 · Burnley Road, Todmorden, West Yorkshire · Close encounter
UNRESOLVED
No accepted explanation

Before dawn, PC Alan Godfrey was driving his patrol car along Burnley Road looking for missing cattle when he found a large, diamond-shaped object hovering about 5 ft above the road, blocking it. He sketched it from the car. The next thing he knew, he was farther down the road and some time had passed. He said the road surface under the object was dry though the rest was wet. Other officers reported a light in the sky that night. Later hypnosis sessions produced an abduction account.

What was seen

Shape: Diamond-shaped object hovering over the road
Witnesses: PC Alan Godfrey; other officers saw a light that night
Duration: Minutes; ~15 minutes unaccounted for
