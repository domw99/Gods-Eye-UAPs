Falcon Lake incident

20 May 1967 · Falcon Lake, Whiteshell Provincial Park, Manitoba · Landing / trace case
DISPUTED
Explanation proposed but contested
Open on the 3D globe →

Amateur prospector Stefan Michalak said two glowing craft descended near him; one landed on a rock outcrop. When he touched it, a blast of hot gas from a grid-like vent burned his chest. CBC has called it Canada’s best-documented UFO case.

Explanation

RCMP, RCAF and the Condon Committee could not explain it; some soil samples were mildly radioactive, possibly from radium paint. Later marks on Michalak’s body were judged self-inflicted.

What was seen

Shape: Two glowing oval objects; one landed
Witnesses: Stefan Michalak
Duration: About 30 minutes
