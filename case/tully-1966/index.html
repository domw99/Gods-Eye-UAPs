Tully "saucer nest"

19 January 1966 · Horseshoe Lagoon near Tully, Queensland · Landing / trace case
DISPUTED
Explanation proposed but contested
Open on the 3D globe →

George Pedley heard a hissing sound and saw a saucer-shaped object rise from a lagoon and climb away. In its place was a floating circle of flattened, clockwise-swirled reeds about 9 m across — the original "saucer nest".

Explanation

Police and the RAAF suggested a willy-willy (whirlwind) flattened the reeds; others considered it unexplained.

What was seen

Shape: Grey saucer rising from the lagoon
Witnesses: Banana grower George Pedley
Duration: Seconds
