Tremonton, Utah film

2 July 1952 · Near Tremonton, Utah · Photo / video case
DISPUTED
Explanation proposed but contested
Open on the 3D globe →

Driving about seven miles north of Tremonton, Navy photographer Delbert Newhouse stopped and filmed a group of bright objects with his Bell & Howell 16 mm camera. The Navy’s Photographic Interpretation Laboratory spent over 1,000 man-hours on it and ruled out aircraft and birds; the Robertson Panel disagreed.

Explanation

Navy photo analysts could not identify the objects; the 1953 Robertson Panel suggested seagulls reflecting sunlight. The film was re-released in 2026 as PURSUE record DOW-UAP-PR159.

What was seen

Shape: About a dozen bright objects milling in the sky
Witnesses: Navy Chief Warrant Officer Delbert C. Newhouse and family
Duration: ~1 min of 16 mm Kodachrome
