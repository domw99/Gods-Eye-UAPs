Torpo film

13 July 1986 · Mountain farm near Torpo, Hallingdal · Photo / video case
UNRESOLVED
No accepted explanation
Open on the 3D globe →

A couple at a mountain farm near Torpo, with their son and friends, watched two bright objects in the evening sky, one moving dramatically, flashing and throwing off sparks. The husband filmed them. Ground Saucer Watch found no sign of a hoax, but said the apparent motion could come from camera shake and that the lack of reference points made the film inconclusive.

What was seen

Shape: Two bright objects, one flashing and throwing sparks
Witnesses: A family and friends at a mountain farm
Duration: Minutes
