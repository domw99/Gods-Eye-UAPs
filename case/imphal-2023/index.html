Imphal airport UFO — Rafales scrambled

19 November 2023 · Bir Tikendrajit International Airport, Imphal, Manipur · Military encounter
UNRESOLVED
No accepted explanation

An unidentified object seen near Imphal airport in the afternoon led the authorities to halt flights. Departures were delayed and arrivals diverted for about three hours. The Indian Air Force scrambled two Rafale fighters from a nearby base, which searched the area and found nothing. The object was not seen again.

What was seen

Shape: Small white object
Witnesses: Airport staff and officials
Duration: About 3 hours of disruption
