Foo fighters — 415th Night Fighter Squadron

23 November 1944 · Rhine valley near Strasbourg · Military encounter
UNRESOLVED
No accepted explanation
Open on the 3D globe →

Crews of the U.S. 415th Night Fighter Squadron repeatedly reported glowing balls of light that followed their Beaufighters over the Rhine. They called them "foo fighters"; similar reports came from Allied and Axis crews in Europe and the Pacific.

Explanation

Proposed explanations include St. Elmo’s fire, ball lightning, flares and misidentified aircraft; none fits all reports.

What was seen

Shape: Balls of orange or red light pacing aircraft
Witnesses: USAAF night-fighter crews
Duration: Recurring, late 1944 – 1945
