Washington, D.C. radar-visual sightings

19 July 1952 · Washington, D.C. · Radar-visual case
DISPUTED
Explanation proposed but contested

On consecutive Saturday nights, controllers at Washington National Airport and Andrews AFB tracked unknown targets over the capital — including the prohibited airspace over the White House and Capitol — while airline pilots saw lights where radar showed them. F-94 jets scrambled from New Castle AFB, Delaware; one pilot said lights surrounded his aircraft. The Pentagon held its largest press conference since WWII.

Explanation

The Air Force attributed the radar returns to temperature inversions; radar controllers involved rejected that explanation. The widely published "Capitol UFO" photo shows lens flares.

What was seen

Shape: Radar blips; orange-red lights
Witnesses: Air traffic controllers, airline and fighter pilots
Duration: Two weekends
