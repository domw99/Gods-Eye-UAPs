Kecksburg fireball

9 December 1965 · Kecksburg, Pennsylvania · Tracked object event
DISPUTED
Explanation proposed but contested
Open on the 3D globe →

A brilliant fireball crossed the sky over the Detroit–Windsor area and was reported from Ontario to Pennsylvania. Residents of Kecksburg said something fell into the woods; state police and military personnel searched and reported finding nothing.

Explanation

Astronomers identified a bright bolide seen from six states and Ontario; no object was found at Kecksburg. Claims of a recovered acorn-shaped craft are unsupported, and the Soviet Kosmos 96 re-entry was ruled out by timing.

What was seen

Shape: Fireball; later claims of an acorn-shaped object
Witnesses: Thousands across six states
Duration: Seconds (fireball)
