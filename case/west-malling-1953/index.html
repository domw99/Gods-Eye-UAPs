West Malling Vampire sighting

3 November 1953 · Over RAF West Malling, Kent · Aviation encounter
DISPUTED
Explanation proposed but contested

Two RAF aircrew flying a de Havilland Vampire over West Malling reported an unidentified object. The report reached the House of Commons, where the Air Minister called it a weather balloon. Another MP raised it again the next day.

Explanation

In Parliament, the Secretary of State for Air said the object was "a weather balloon". The crew did not accept this.
