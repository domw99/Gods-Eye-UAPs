RAF Topcliffe disc — Exercise Mainbrace

19 September 1952 · RAF Topcliffe / Dishforth, North Yorkshire · Military encounter
UNRESOLVED
No accepted explanation
Open on the 3D globe →

During NATO's Exercise Mainbrace, RAF aircrew at Topcliffe watched a silver, disc-shaped object following a Meteor jet as it came down to land at nearby Dishforth. The object descended, swinging "like a pendulum", hovered and rotated. It then shot away westward at high speed. The report made front pages and was raised in Parliament years later.

What was seen

Shape: Silver disc, rotating
Witnesses: Flt Lt John Kilburn and RAF aircrew on the ground
Duration: About 20 seconds of hovering
