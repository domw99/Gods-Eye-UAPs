# Code of conduct

This project documents a subject people feel strongly about. It works only if everyone can argue about evidence without being attacked.

**Be kind to people; be hard on claims.** Disagree with a source, a status or an explanation as much as you like, and say why. Do not insult, mock or demean the people who made a sighting report, investigated one, or are reading this.

**Stick to evidence.** Back a correction with a source. Do not use the issue tracker to promote a belief, a product or a person, and do not post other people's private information.

**No harassment.** That includes personal attacks, threats, hate speech, sexual language or images, and following someone from thread to thread.

**Respect witnesses.** Cases name witnesses only where the sources do and the information is public record. If a person named in a case asks to be removed or corrected, open an issue and it will be looked at promptly.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who do not follow this. To report a problem with someone's behaviour, use the repository's private [security advisory form](https://github.com/domw99/Gods-Eye-UAPs/security/advisories/new) and say it is a conduct report, or open an issue asking for contact without details.

This applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere you represent the project.
