GOD'S EYE // UAP

Open data: 161 UFO/UAP case files

161 well-documented UFO/UAP encounters from 1561 to 2024 in 33 countries and territories. For each: the date and place with coordinates, the kind of encounter, the evidence, the official or best explanation and its status, a short summary and timeline, and the sources. 70 cases have reconstructed flight paths, each marked with what it is based on (radar, an official report, witness reports or an approximate reconstruction). Made for the God's Eye // UAP 3D globe.

Version 1.9, built 2026-10-07. Free to use under the MIT licence, like the rest of the repository; the media and archives the sources link to keep their own licences. Please credit “God's Eye // UAP” and link back.

Files

  • cases.json · Every field, with the flight paths and timelines.
  • cases.csv · One row per case, for spreadsheets. Lists are joined with “ | ”; flight paths are counted, not included.
  • cases.geojson · A point per case and a 3D line per flight path, for GIS tools and web maps.

Fields

idStable identifier; the case page is case/<id>/
date, timeApproximateLocal date and time with its UTC offset, as recorded (ISO 8601); timeApproximate is true when the time of day is a guess
place, country, countryCodeWhere it happened; ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 country code
latitude, longitude, locationPrecisionWGS 84 degrees; how exact the position is: site, city, area, region
categoryThe kind of encounter: military-encounter, aviation, radar-visual, mass-sighting, photo-video, landing-trace, close-encounter, recurring-lights, object-event, historical
status, statusLabel, explanationWhat is known about the cause: unresolved, disputed, explained, identified, unassessed
evidenceThe kinds of evidence on record (video, radar, official documents, witnesses…)
shape, witnesses, duration, summary, timelineWhat was seen, by whom, for how long, and in what order
tracksReconstructed flight paths: [longitude, latitude, altitude in metres, seconds from the start]; basis: radar, official-report, witness-reports, reconstruction, flight-plan, approximate
sources, wikipediaWhere the facts come from
page, globeThe case page, and the case on the 3D globe

Example

fetch('https://domw99.github.io/Gods-Eye-UAPs/open-data/cases.json')
  .then((r) => r.json())
  .then(({ records }) => records.filter((c) => c.status === 'unresolved'));

Accuracy

Positions follow the sources and are only as exact as locationPrecision says. Flight paths are reconstructions; each says what it is based on, and “approximate” ones are drawn from descriptions. A status says what the evidence supports, not what anyone believes. Corrections are welcome as issues on GitHub.
